The other half of training

Conditioning

Cardio has three jobs here: burn extra calories, build the engine that makes you look athletic, and keep your heart healthy. The whole art is dosing it so it speeds fat loss without stealing recovery from your lifts.

How to add weight

Double progression, and nothing else. Add reps until you hit the top of the range on every set, then add the smallest jump and start again at the bottom.

What happenedWhat you do next session
Hit the sets, below the top of the rangeSame weight, chase one more rep
Top of the range on every set+2.5 kg upper / +5 kg lower, reps reset to the bottom
Reps dropped or the bar slowedRepeat it clean before you add anything
Stalled 2–3 weeksCheck sleep, protein, rest between sets — then cut 10% and rebuild
In a deficit, holding your lifts is already a win. Weight on the bar going up while the scale goes down is the recomp working.
