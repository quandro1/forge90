The other half of training

Conditioning

Cardio has three jobs here: it burns extra calories, builds your fitness, and keeps your heart healthy. The dose is kept small enough that it never takes recovery away from your lifting.

How to add weight

Double progression, and nothing else. Add reps until you hit the top of the range on every set, then add the smallest jump and start again at the bottom.

What happenedWhat you do next session
Hit the sets, below the top of the rangeSame weight, chase one more rep
Top of the range on every setAdd the smallest jump the equipment allows (the logbook names it); reps go back to the bottom
Reps dropped or the bar slowedRepeat it clean before you add anything
Stalled 2–3 weeksCheck sleep, protein, rest between sets — then cut 10% and rebuild
If you are eating less to lose fat, holding your lifts is already a win. The bar going up while the scale goes down means you are losing fat, not muscle.
